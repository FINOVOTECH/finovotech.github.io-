# How to open accounts using DigiLocker in India

Imagine it's mid-April, and your brokerage in Mumbai has its hands full with the end-of-financial-year rush. Every minute saved in processing client documents feels like a win. Here's where DigiLocker steps in as a game-changer.

## What is DigiLocker?

DigiLocker, launched by MeitY, is an Indian digital document wallet that provides Indian citizens with a secure way to store and share documents online. As financial institutions look to streamline their account opening processes, DigiLocker offers a compliant, efficient way to retrieve and verify client documentation. It integrates seamlessly with eKYC systems, reducing manual verification time and improving accuracy.

## Why use DigiLocker for account opening?

Using DigiLocker for account opening simplifies the verification process, making it more convenient for both customers and institutions. Here's how:

1. **Document authenticity**: Documents fetched via DigiLocker come directly from issuing authorities ensuring authenticity.
2. **Efficiency**: Reduces time spent on manual document checks, speeding up the onboarding process.
3. **Cost-effective**: Minimizes paperwork, storage, and verification costs by integrating digital methods.

## Steps to open an account using DigiLocker

1. **Integration setup**: Financial institutions need to integrate their systems with DigiLocker's API. This integration ensures seamless retrieval of documents like Aadhaar, PAN, and more.
2. **Customer onboarding**: When onboarding a customer, the broker or NBFC can request document access through DigiLocker.
3. **Document verification**: Once the customer consents, their documents are fetched in real-time and verified automatically.
4. **Account approval**: With verified documents, the account can be approved rapidly, benefiting the customer and the institution.

## Overcoming common challenges

Though DigiLocker simplifies many processes, certain challenges might arise:

- **Customer awareness**: Some customers may not be familiar with DigiLocker and its benefits. It falls on financial institutions to educate them during onboarding.
- **Seamless tech setup**: Ensuring seamless integration with existing systems might require some initial setup efforts from your IT team.
