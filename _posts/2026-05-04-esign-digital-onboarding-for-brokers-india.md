# how esign digital onboarding is reshaping India’s brokerage

Imagine a broker in India working late at night to finalize onboarding for a new client. Traditional paperwork makes this a tedious process, but with esign digital onboarding, things move more smoothly. Focused on compliance and efficiency, this technology helps brokers keep up with growing regulatory demands.

## why use esign technology in broker onboarding?

Esign technology simplifies the onboarding process for brokers in India. It aligns with SEBI compliance standards, reducing the hassle of paperwork and minimizing mistakes. This makes the process not just efficient but also secure, enhancing client satisfaction significantly.

### benefits of esign onboarding
- **Speed and convenience**: Clients can sign documents instantly, accelerating the opening of a brokerage account.
- **Compliance assured**: Every esign action meets the digital standards of regulators like SEBI.
- **Reduction of human error**: Digital signatures reduce errors often seen in manual processing.

## esign: aligning with regulatory demands

Meeting SEBI regulations is crucial for brokers. Esign digital onboarding ensures brokers remain compliant by automatically capturing and storing signing events, which properly documents all necessary processes.

## enhance broker-client relationships with esign

Digital onboarding with esign strengthens broker-client relationships. The faster the process, the sooner brokers can begin offering valuable services, creating an easy transition from prospect to active investor.
