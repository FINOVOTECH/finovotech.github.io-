# Designing effective eKYC solutions for stock brokers

Picture your brokerage firm swamped with new account applications after a market boom. Tackling these while staying compliant can feel overwhelming. That's where enterprise eKYC solutions come in, simplifying onboarding while ensuring regulatory compliance.

## eKYC vs video KYC for brokers

Traditional manual KYC processes can be tedious and slow, creating bottlenecks. eKYC offers a digital alternative that aligns with current technology trends. By digitizing KYC, brokers ensure compliance with SEBI KRA norms and significantly cut down onboarding times. Contrast this with video KYC, which, though it adds face-to-face verification, can unnecessarily complicate workflows.

Thinking about [WhatsApp eKYC](/services#whatsapp)? It's a practical complement to eKYC, especially for verifying identities remotely.

## Brokerage onboarding workflow automation

In today's landscape, automating the onboarding workflow is essential for any brokerage aiming to be efficient. Old systems are not only slow but also prone to errors. With tools like [Finovo CRM](/services#crm), brokers can automate each stage of the client journey, from initial contact to compliance checks.

Imagine automating [eIPO integration](/services#eipo) and document verification through Aadhaar XML. This streamlines onboarding, reduces paperwork, and ensures compliance with POA and POI requirements.

## Benefits of Re-KYC for brokers

Many brokers underestimate the cost of missing Re-KYC deadlines. The requirement for updates every six months is challenging but crucial. Staying on top of Re-KYC prevents business interruptions and avoids fines.

If a significant portion of your clients—around 40%—haven't completed Re-KYC, our [rekyc solution](/services#rekyc) can assist in meeting SEBI regulations while improving the client experience.

## Integrated eKYC solutions for brokers in India

Choosing the right eKYC solution matters. While a low-cost option might seem appealing, investing in a comprehensive solution often proves more beneficial. An integrated system ensures efficient operations and compliance.

Considering an upgrade? [Enterprise eKYC systems](/services#ekyc-enterprise) help balance cost and functionality, tailored for brokerage needs to ensure smooth integration.

### Low-cost eKYC vendors

Differentiate between inexpensive and cost-effective solutions. While low-cost vendors may cut initial expenses, they often struggle with updates and integration, leading to hidden costs in efficiency. Always align vendor choices with your operational and compliance goals.

## How to comply with SEBI KRA norms for brokers

Compliance is more than ticking boxes—it's a continuous effort. SEBI's norms require vigilance and adaptability. Automated compliance software eases the manual workload, protecting your firm from penalties.
