## Why language matters in eKYC

India's linguistic landscape offers challenges and opportunities. For financial bodies like Findoc, multilingual eKYC means wider accessibility. In a nation where sticking to one language can alienate many potential customers, this is vital. Think about a client who feels more at ease completing KYC in Gujarati or Marathi instead of English. Enterprise eKYC addresses this, making sure language doesn't block compliance or engagement.
