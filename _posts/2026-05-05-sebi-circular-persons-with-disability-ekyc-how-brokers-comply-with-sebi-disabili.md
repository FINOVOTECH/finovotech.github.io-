# SEBI guidelines for eKYC: meeting disability norms

Picture this: you're a broker and SEBI's newest circular has landed on your desk. It outlines strict new video eKYC guidelines for onboarding people with disabilities. With the deadline looming in April 2024, brokers in India must adapt their eKYC processes to meet these inclusive standards or face the risk of regulatory setbacks and losing market competitiveness.

## Understanding SEBI's disability eKYC mandate

SEBI's 2024 directive is clear: brokers are required to incorporate an eKYC process that supports people with disabilities. This is mandatory, not merely suggested, to ensure everyone has equal access to financial services. Brokers must shift from traditional practices, which often overlook key user groups, to comply with this mandate.

## Video eKYC benefits for disability access

Video eKYC isn't just about compliance. It provides genuine access for individuals needing personalized onboarding. Visual verification ensures accurate identity checks without compromising the user experience for those with disabilities. It reduces errors common in document-heavy methods, making the process clearer and more accessible.
