## Understanding the need for specific eKYC processes

Before diving into specifics, it's crucial to understand why the eKYC process for NRE and NRO accounts is distinct. Non-Resident External (NRE) and Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO) accounts cater to non-resident Indians, thus requiring additional compliance with international banking regulations and strict adherence to SEBI’s rules. This calls for a digital solution that not only meets these regulatory requirements but also provides a seamless experience for non-resident clients.
